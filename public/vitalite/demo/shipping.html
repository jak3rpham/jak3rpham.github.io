Shipping

Where we ship, what it costs, how long it takes.

Last updated 30 August 2026

On this page

Vietnam United States Damage in transit

Vietnam

Orders inside Vietnam ship with SPX. Shipping is 30,000₫, and free on orders of three pieces or more. The exact cost appears at checkout before you pay, and nothing is added to the total afterwards.

WhereDelivery time
Ho Chi Minh City1 day
Everywhere else in Vietnam1 to 3 days

Cash on delivery is available inside Vietnam. You pay the courier when the parcel is handed to you.

United States

We ship to the United States. Orders arrive in one to two weeks.

Shipping is calculated at checkout and shown before you pay.

Damage in transit

Please check the parcel when it is handed to you. Signing for a delivery without noting a problem is treated as accepting the order in the condition it arrived.

If something is wrong, the unboxing video described in our returns policy is what lets us make a claim with the carrier on your behalf.
